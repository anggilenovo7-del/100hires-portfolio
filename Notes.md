# 1. Purpose

Explain the objective of this playbook.

Example:

This playbook provides a practical framework for building a sustainable LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS companies. It combines insights gathered from 10 experienced practitioners and focuses on creating long-term demand rather than short-term engagement.

# 2. Scope

This playbook covers:

- Audience research
- Positioning
- Personal branding
- Content creation
- Publishing
- Engagement
- Distribution
- Measurement
- AI-assisted workflow

This playbook does NOT cover:

- Paid LinkedIn Ads
- Cold outreach
- Sales prospecting

# 4. Success Metrics

Define what success looks like.

Primary Metrics

- Qualified impressions
- Saves
- Meaningful comments
- Profile visits
- Newsletter subscribers
- Demo requests
- Pipeline generated

Avoid Vanity Metrics

- Followers only
- Likes only
- Viral posts without business impact
